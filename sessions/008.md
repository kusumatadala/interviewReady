# Session 008 — Behavioral — first STAR + mini-mock

**40 minutes · Core foundations · No assigned calendar date**

**Goal:** Tell authentic technical stories that survive follow-up questions.  
**Prerequisite:** Session 007 or a documented equivalent skill.  
**Sources:** [Google re:Work — structured interviewing](https://rework.withgoogle.com/) · [STAR interview technique](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/interview-advice/the-star-method)

## 1. Recall / learn — 5 minutes
- [ ] Review one genuine production incident or delivery improvement you helped make.
- [ ] Separate your personal actions from the team's actions; omit confidential details.

## 2. Hands-on practice — 25 minutes
- [ ] Write a STAR outline: Situation 2 sentences, Task 1, Action 3–4 specific choices, Result measurable only if you can substantiate.
- [ ] Speak the story in 90 seconds and record audio locally if comfortable; avoid exaggerating responsibility.
- [ ] Practice a follow-up asking about alternatives, mistakes, test evidence and what you'd change.
- [ ] Spend five minutes explaining why a Kafka consumer's idempotency key must be checked transactionally.

## 3. Verify / explain — 10 minutes
- [ ] Score story 0–5 for clarity, ownership, decisions, evidence and learning.
- [ ] Score technical explanation 0–5 for delivery vs business effects, unique key, transaction, crash recovery and monitoring.

**Definition of done:** One accurate 90-second story and one 2-minute technical explanation with self-scores.

**Expected evidence:** Private STAR notes and numeric score (public-safe summary optional)

### Mock interview check
**Question:** Tell me about an engineering decision you revisited after new evidence.

**Answer should cover:**
- [ ] Truthful original decision
- [ ] Observed impact
- [ ] Ownership without blame
- [ ] Recovery actions
- [ ] What changed afterward
